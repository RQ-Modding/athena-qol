# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.0.1

- The Studies' descriptions say the world's gain (Attack Studies: "Athena's attack will increase
  by 100 after each battle" in world 1, 500 in world 2, 2000 in world 3), not the game's +3.
- Athena Maximum's description: "Heals 10% HP each turn", and no mention of Damage Maximum.

## 1.0.0

First version, for Athena 2.0.
- Five skill slots (Skill Slots ★★).
- Any bow, whatever its level.
- Studies by world: attack and HP +100 / 500 / 2000, accuracy, speed and critical +5 / 10 / 20.
- One stored skill point at world 2 and one more at world 3.
- Smarter Aim (world 2, 1 point): Ranged Attack 1 to 3 become Calculated Shot 1 to 3, with twice
  the damage limit.
- Athena Maximum (world 3, 1 point, yellow): with a bow, damage limits +100%, Calculated Shot on a
  known weakness, 10% HP at each of her turns, +40 accuracy.
